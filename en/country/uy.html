What the flag means

The "Sun of May" in the upper left is said to be a symbol of the movement for independence from Spain. The white and blue colors are also linked to the flag of Artigas, a leader of the independence movement.

Fun facts

  • FlagThe 9 stripes on Uruguay’s flag represent the 9 departments at the time of independence.
  • GeographyUruguay is a gently rolling country, and even its highest point is only about 500 m above sea level.
  • CultureUruguay’s official name is the “Oriental Republic of Uruguay,” and “Oriental” means east of the Uruguay River.
  • CultureThe 1st FIFA World Cup was held in Uruguay in 1930, and the hosts, Uruguay, won it.
