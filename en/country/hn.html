What the flag means

The blue bands at the top and bottom are said to stand for the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. The white in the center is said to stand for the land between them and for peace. The blue-white-blue pattern was inherited from the flag of the former Federal Republic of Central America.

Fun facts

  • FlagThe 5 stars on the flag of Honduras represent the 5 countries of the former Federal Republic of Central America.
  • GeographyThe Bay Islands of Honduras, in the Caribbean Sea, are a famous diving spot with beautiful coral reefs.
  • CultureThe name Honduras means “depths” in Spanish.
  • CultureThe ruins of Copán in Honduras have a “Hieroglyphic Stairway” densely carved with Maya script.
