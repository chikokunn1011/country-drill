What the flag means

Red is said to stand for national unity and victory, white for cotton and purity, and green for rich nature and valleys. The crown stands for the Tajik people, and it is often explained by linking the name "Tajik" with "taj" (crown).

Fun facts

  • FlagIn the center of Tajikistan’s flag is a crown with 7 stars arranged in an arc above it.
  • GeographyMore than 90% of Tajikistan is mountainous, and the Pamirs, the “Roof of the World,” spread across it.
  • CultureDushanbe, the capital of Tajikistan, means “Monday” in Tajik.
  • CultureTajik, the official language of Tajikistan, is very close to Persian.
