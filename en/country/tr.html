What the flag means

Red is said to stand for the blood of those who died for the country. A legend tells that the flag came from a crescent and star reflected in a pool of blood after a battle.

Fun facts

  • FlagTurkey’s flag, a white crescent and star on a red field, is called “Ay Yıldız” (moon star).
  • GeographyIstanbul, Turkey’s largest city, straddles Europe and Asia on both sides of the Bosporus.
  • CultureTurkey’s capital is not Istanbul but Ankara, an inland city.
  • CultureSaint Nicholas, the model for Santa Claus, was the bishop of Myra, a town in what is now southern Turkey.
