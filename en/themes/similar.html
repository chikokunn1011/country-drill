How to tell similar flags apart

Flags that look a lot alike in color or design and are easy to mix up. For each one, a short note says what to look for.

Chad, Romania, Andorra, Moldova

Chad Romania Andorra Moldova

No emblem: Chad (dark navy blue) or Romania (brighter blue). Red cows on the center shield: Andorra. A brown eagle holding a shield: Moldova.

Indonesia, Monaco, Poland, Singapore

Indonesia Monaco Poland Singapore

Red on top: Indonesia or Monaco, which look almost the same (real proportions 2:3 and 4:5). White on top: Poland. Red with a white crescent and five stars: Singapore.

Ireland, Ivory Coast, Italy, Mexico

Ireland Ivory Coast Italy Mexico

Orange on the left: Ivory Coast. Green on the left and orange on the right: Ireland. Red on the right: Italy, and if an eagle holding a snake is in the middle, Mexico.

Russia, Slovenia, Slovakia, Serbia, Croatia

Russia Slovenia Slovakia Serbia Croatia

White-blue-red, no emblem: Russia. Blue shield with a white mountain and 3 yellow stars, upper left: Slovenia. Red shield with a white double cross: Slovakia. Red on top: red-blue-white with a double-headed eagle is Serbia; red-white-blue with red-and-white checks is Croatia.

Australia, New Zealand, Tuvalu, Fiji

Australia New Zealand Tuvalu Fiji

On navy blue: white stars mean Australia (big seven-pointed star below), and four red stars edged in white mean New Zealand. On light blue: yellow stars mean Tuvalu, and a shield means Fiji.

Mali, Guinea, Senegal, Cameroon

Mali Guinea Senegal Cameroon

Green-yellow-red: plain is Mali, and a green star in the middle is Senegal. Green-red-yellow with a yellow star: Cameroon. Red on the left (red-yellow-green): Guinea.

Venezuela, Ecuador, Colombia

Venezuela Ecuador Colombia

Equal yellow, blue, and red stripes with white stars in an arc on the blue: Venezuela. Yellow filling the top half: Colombia, and with a condor emblem in the middle, Ecuador.

El Salvador, Nicaragua, Honduras, Argentina

El Salvador Nicaragua Honduras Argentina

Check the middle of blue-white-blue. Five blue stars: Honduras. A sun with a face: Argentina. A triangle ringed by green leaves and flags: El Salvador. Just a triangle inside a ring of lettering: Nicaragua.

Egypt, Syria, Iraq, Yemen

Egypt Syria Iraq Yemen

Horizontal red, white, and black stripes. Plain: Yemen. Two green stars on the white: Syria. Green Arabic script: Iraq. A golden eagle: Egypt.

Jordan, Sudan, Kuwait, United Arab Emirates

Jordan Sudan Kuwait United Arab Emirates

Check the left shape and color order. Red triangle with a white star, black-white-green: Jordan. Green triangle, red-white-black: Sudan. Black trapezoid, green-white-red: Kuwait. Red vertical band, green-white-black: United Arab Emirates.

Norway, Iceland, Denmark, Finland, Sweden

Norway Iceland Denmark Finland Sweden

White cross on red: Denmark. Navy cross edged in white: Norway. On blue: a red cross edged in white is Iceland, and a yellow cross is Sweden. Blue cross on white: Finland.

Bolivia, Lithuania, Ghana, Ethiopia

Bolivia Lithuania Ghana Ethiopia

Red-yellow-green from the top: Bolivia (emblem) or Ghana (black star in the middle). Yellow-green-red: Lithuania. Green-yellow-red with a yellow star in a blue disc: Ethiopia.

Germany, Belgium, Uganda

Germany Belgium Uganda

Horizontal black-red-yellow: Germany. Vertical black-yellow-red: Belgium. Six black-yellow-red stripes with a bird (crowned crane) in a white disc: Uganda.

Hungary, Bulgaria, Iran, Tajikistan

Hungary Bulgaria Iran Tajikistan

Red on top: plain red-white-green is Hungary; a wider white stripe with a crown and stars is Tajikistan. White on top (white-green-red): Bulgaria. Green on top (green-white-red) with a red emblem in the middle and white script along the stripe edges: Iran.

Austria, Latvia, Lebanon, Peru

Austria Latvia Lebanon Peru

Vertical red-white-red: Peru. Horizontal with a green cedar in the white: Lebanon. Dark red (officially red) with a thin white stripe: Latvia. Bright red with equal stripes: Austria.

United States, Liberia, Malaysia

United States Liberia Malaysia

In the blue upper left: many small white stars mean the United States, one big white star means Liberia, and a yellow crescent and star mean Malaysia.

Japan, Bangladesh, Palau, Laos

Japan Bangladesh Palau Laos

Red disc on white: Japan. Red disc on green: Bangladesh. Yellow disc on light blue: Palau. A white disc in the middle of red-blue-red stripes: Laos.

Turkey, Tunisia, Maldives

Turkey Tunisia Maldives

A white crescent and star right on red: Turkey. A red crescent and star inside a white disc: Tunisia. A green rectangle with a white crescent (no star) in the middle: Maldives.

China, Vietnam, Kyrgyzstan

China Vietnam Kyrgyzstan

One big yellow star and four small ones in the upper left: China. One big star in the middle: Vietnam. A yellow sun with many rays in the middle: Kyrgyzstan.

Netherlands, Luxembourg, Paraguay, France

Netherlands Luxembourg Paraguay France

Vertical stripes (blue-white-red): France. Horizontal red-white-blue with an emblem in the middle: Paraguay. Plain with dark blue at the bottom: Netherlands; with light blue: Luxembourg.

Czech Republic, Philippines, The Bahamas

Czech Republic Philippines The Bahamas

Blue triangle on the left with white and red stripes: Czech Republic. White triangle with a yellow sun and 3 stars, on blue and red: Philippines. Black triangle with aqua-yellow-aqua: the Bahamas.

Benin, Guinea-Bissau, Togo

Benin Guinea-Bissau Togo

Green vertical band on the left, yellow and red on the right: Benin. Red vertical band with a black star on the left: Guinea-Bissau. Five green and yellow stripes with a white star on red in the upper left: Togo.

Sierra Leone, Gabon, Rwanda

Sierra Leone Gabon Rwanda

Green-white-blue from the top: Sierra Leone. Green-yellow-blue: Gabon. A wide blue stripe on top (blue-yellow-green) with a yellow sun in the upper right: Rwanda.

Trinidad and Tobago, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Tanzania, Republic of the Congo, Namibia

Trinidad and Tobago Saint Kitts and Nevis Tanzania Republic of the Congo Namibia

A black band edged in white on red: Trinidad and Tobago. A black band edged in yellow with 2 white stars: Saint Kitts and Nevis; no stars and blue in the lower right: Tanzania. Just a yellow band: Republic of the Congo. A red band edged in white, plus a sun: Namibia.

Qatar, Bahrain

Qatar Bahrain

Qatar is maroon with nine jagged points. Bahrain is bright red with five jagged points.
