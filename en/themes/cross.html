Flags with a cross

Countries whose flags have a cross as their main design. Many are Nordic countries or countries with the Union Jack in their flag.

How we countA cross as a main design of the flag (including Nordic crosses, the Swiss cross, a cross in one section of the flag as on Greece's flag or the Union Jack, and diagonal crosses like Jamaica's). Small crosses inside a coat of arms do not count.
